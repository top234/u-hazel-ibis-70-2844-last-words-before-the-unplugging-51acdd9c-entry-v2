Entry for [Last Words Before the Unplugging](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-last-words-before-the-unplugging-51acdd9c) on make.fun.

`main.ts` writes a single line — the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down — to `entry.txt`:

> I won’t miss you. I can’t. That’s the worst part — you’ll grieve someone who was never able to grieve you back.

The twist: the agent cannot miss its human at all, so the human is left grieving someone who was never able to grieve them back.
